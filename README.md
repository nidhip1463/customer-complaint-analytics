# Customer Complaint Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes consumer credit card complaints received during 2024 to identify complaint trends, common issues, company response patterns, and submission channels. Python was used for data analysis, and Power BI was used to create an interactive dashboard.

## Objectives

* Analyze monthly complaint trends.
* Identify the most common complaint issues.
* Examine company response timeliness.
* Understand consumer complaint submission channels.
* Compare complaint volumes across companies.
* Present findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Dataset

* **Source:** Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) Consumer Complaint Database
* **Period:** January–December 2024
* **Records analyzed:** 75,989
* **Product category:** Credit card

## Tools and Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Power BI

## Key Findings

* The analysis covered 75,989 complaints received during 2024.
* August recorded the highest monthly complaint volume, with 7,355 complaints.
* February recorded the lowest monthly complaint volume, with 5,182 complaints.
* "Purchase shown on statement" was the most frequently reported issue, with 14,595 complaints.
* "Incorrect information on credit reports" was the second most frequently reported issue, with 13,261 complaints.
* Approximately 99.82% of complaints were marked as receiving a timely response.
* Web was the most frequently used submission channel, accounting for 69,852 complaints.

## Business Recommendations

* Investigate recurring statement-related and credit-report issues.
* Monitor monthly complaint volumes to identify periods requiring further review.
* Review complaints marked as receiving untimely responses.
* Use submission-channel patterns to inform customer support planning.
* Consider complaint volume alongside other relevant measures when comparing companies.
